
NNN — November 2026 Challenge Tracker

Welcome to the official tracking platform for the November 2026 challenge. This application is a self-hosted dashboard built to help participants log their daily progress, view global streaks, protect community milestones, and share verified success cards with peers.

🌟 Key Application Features

• One-Click Google Authentication: Seamless sign-in and secure account creation using your existing Google account credentials.
• Daily Verification Grid: A responsive dashboard interface to quickly mark off your current challenge day.
• Leaderboard and Streaks Engine: Live rank metrics showcasing current streaks, historic milestones, and total verified active participants across the community.
• Locked Timezone Integrity: Automatically logs operations using your local tracking region anchor. Account protection rules lock your timezone context daily to preserve legitimate streaks and maintain fair positioning on the public boards.
• Custom Share Cards: Instantly generate clean, dynamic progression summary graphics to share across social platforms.

📱 User Interaction Flow


1. Account Onboarding

When logging into the interface for the first time, you will be prompted to pick a unique community handle (3 to 20 characters using letters, numbers, or underscores) and select your active tracking timezone.

2. Daily Logging and Undoing

• Select the current day square highlighted on your dashboard matrix grid.
• Triggering a day square safely updates your calendar matrix and unlocks your day rewards panel.
• If you make an accidental check-in, hit the undo button directly from the interface within the active tracking window to adjust your daily log status instantly.

3. Reviewing Rewards

Checking off an open day successfully reveals short-lived streaming clips served straight from the private community archive directly inside your pop-up module panel.
